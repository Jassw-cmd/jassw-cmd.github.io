2026 · ongoing · research

Building surrogate models that approximate expensive simulations, then applying causal analysis to understand what drives system behaviour. Part of a digital twin direction that extends toward cognitive twins.

2026 · ongoing · research

Studying graph-based RAG, starting from Microsoft's “From Local to Global” GraphRAG approach, and exploring how digital twin and physical-AI ideas can improve retrieval and answer quality.

2026 · product concept

A children's instant camera that uses computer vision to run topic quests (find a flower → find a rose) and prints magnetic photos that stack into collections. I designed the concept and a software visualisation of the experience.
